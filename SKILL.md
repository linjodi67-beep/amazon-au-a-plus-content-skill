# Amazon AU A+ Content

Use this skill to turn product photos and product details into a reusable Amazon Australia A+ Content set with desktop and mobile image versions.

## Default output

Create a full module set unless the user asks for fewer:

1. Hero lifestyle / first screen
2. Benefit-focused headline + key selling-point icons
3. Multi-angle product view
4. What's included
5. Close-up quality/details
6. Size / pack / fit guide
7. Comparison chart
8. Brand story

For each module, create:

- Desktop image: `1464 x 600 px`
- Mobile image: `600 x 450 px`

Use clear, short English copy suitable for Amazon Australia shoppers. Avoid long paragraphs in images.

## Required source inputs

Extract or ask for:

- Product name
- Item number / SKU
- Brand
- Includes / contents
- GTIN or barcode if supplied
- Packaged dimensions and weight if supplied
- Product description
- Product photos: front, side, back, close-ups, packaging if available
- Brand story source, if requested

If sizing is not supplied, do not invent body measurements. Use safe wording such as "wear over your own basics" or "check listing size information".

## Product image safety

Preserve the original product and model proportions.

- Do not stretch, warp, redraw, or AI-regenerate model faces or bodies.
- Make the product/model large enough to be the visual hero. For hero/lifestyle modules, the visible product/model should normally occupy about 60-85% of the image height on desktop and mobile, unless the product type makes that impossible.
- Crop away unused white/transparent margins before resizing product photos so the real product does not become tiny inside a large empty image box.
- Keep text and product/model in separate safe zones. Product/model must never cover headlines, body copy, icons, comparison tables, labels, or callout cards.
- Inspect the final contact sheet specifically for product scale and text overlap before handing off.
- Prefer clean cutout compositing from the supplied photos.
- Use generative image tools for backgrounds, mood, props, and layouts only when that does not alter the product/model.
- If using AI image editing, explicitly instruct it to keep the supplied product/model unchanged.
- Avoid fake-looking hands, faces, limbs, or costume shape changes.

## Creative direction

Prioritize conversion drivers in this order:

1. Professional lifestyle photography or lifestyle-style background
2. Clear benefit-focused headlines
3. Icons with key selling points
4. Comparison chart
5. What's included section
6. Size / pack guide
7. Close-up quality details
8. Brand story

For costumes and novelty party products, make the visuals playful, bright, and retail-polished. Use the product theme as the creative motif, but keep the layout clean enough for Amazon.

## Copy rules

Use benefit-led headlines. Examples:

- "Serve Up True Blue Aussie Fun"
- "Why Party Guests Remember It"
- "Full Look From Every Angle"
- "What's Included"
- "Close-Up Costume Details"
- "Pack & Product Guide"
- "Choose Your Party Moment"
- "Tomfoolery Brand Story"

Avoid unsupported claims:

- Do not claim exact fit, age range, fabric content, washability, flame resistance, compliance, or durability unless supplied.
- Do not say "best", "#1", "guaranteed", or "official" unless substantiated.
- Do not include price, discount, shipping, reviews, or external website CTAs inside A+ images.
- Do not place GTIN/barcode numbers in customer-facing A+ visuals by default. Keep them for backend notes only unless the user explicitly asks to show them.

## Brand story handling

When using Tomfoolery brand story, compress it into short marketplace-safe points:

- Established in Melbourne in 1962
- Longstanding wholesale/B2B heritage serving Australian and New Zealand retailers
- Recently bringing selected retail products direct to shoppers through Amazon
- Costume, party, souvenir/gift and related-category product know-how
- Melbourne/Hallam-based facilities and experienced distribution team

Frame the story for consumer trust, not just company history. A good direction is: "trusted wholesale roots, now available on Amazon." Explain that years of supplying retailers gives the brand practical product-selection experience, while Amazon makes selected favourites easier for retail shoppers to buy. Paraphrase; do not paste long website blocks into images.

## Workflow

1. Gather product facts and inspect supplied photos.
2. Decide module order and image count.
3. Draft concise module copy first.
4. Generate or select a creative background that matches the product theme.
5. Cut out and composite product photos proportionally.
6. Size the product/model generously, especially in hero and lifestyle modules.
7. Add icons, badges, charts, and text with mobile-specific layout.
8. Check that no product/model or decorative element overlaps text.
9. Export exact desktop/mobile sizes with descriptive filenames.
10. Validate dimensions and visually inspect a contact sheet before handing off.
